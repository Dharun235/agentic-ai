# Company Support Knowledge

_Customer-facing reference. Check the relevant section before answering._

## Returns

Customers can return products within 30 days of purchase.

The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

Returns are reviewed after the warehouse receives the product. Customers should include the order number and use secure packaging.

### Refunds

Refunds are processed within 5 business days after the returned product has been inspected.

Refunds normally go to the original payment method. A refund may appear in the bank account later, depending on the payment provider.

### Return exceptions

- Custom-made products may have separate return rules.
- Digital products may have separate cancellation rules.
- Damaged deliveries should be reported with photographs as soon as possible.
- Promotional bundles may require every bundle item to be returned.

## Technical support

Technical support is available Monday through Friday.

Support hours are 09:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.

Customers can contact technical support by email at support@example.test.

When contacting support, include the product model, order number if available, exact error message, and steps already attempted. Never include passwords, authentication codes, or full payment-card numbers.

### Basic troubleshooting

1. Check power and cable connections.
2. Restart the affected device.
3. Install approved updates.
4. Reproduce the issue with unnecessary accessories disconnected.
5. Escalate unresolved hardware or safety issues.

## Shipping

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days.

Shipping delays may occur during public holidays, severe weather, customs checks, or carrier capacity issues.

Shipping estimates begin after payment confirmation and are estimates, not guarantees.

### Delivery tracking

Tracking may remain unchanged for 24 hours after dispatch. Customers should verify the delivery address before opening a missing-parcel case.

## Orders and payments

Orders normally move through payment pending, confirmed, picking, packed, dispatched, and delivered states.

Customers can request an address correction before dispatch, but the warehouse cannot guarantee changes after picking begins.

A payment authorization may appear before a payment is captured. Two pending authorizations do not necessarily mean two completed charges.

## Account and security

Password reset links expire after 60 minutes. Account closure does not automatically cancel open orders.

Support agents must not request passwords, full payment-card numbers, or authentication codes. Suspected account compromise should be escalated to account security.

## Important terminology

- **Business day:** Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays in the processing location.
- **Dispatch:** The parcel has left a warehouse.
- **Delivered:** The carrier marked the parcel as delivered.
- **Original packaging:** Retail box, inserts, and included protective material when reasonably available.

> Do not infer warranty coverage from shipping status. Warranty claims are separate from ordinary returns.
